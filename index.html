006studio started as a young creative driven by curiosity, passion, and a desire to create.


With 7+ years of experience in the creative industry, we specialize in 2D & 3D Motion Design, turning ideas and visions into clear, distinctive visual experiences.


From Vision to Visual


006studio brings together two creative disciplines: COLORBLIND — 2D & 3D Motion Design; 006LAB — 3D Printing & Object Design.


From digital motion to physical objects, we turn ideas into visuals with character and purpose.
